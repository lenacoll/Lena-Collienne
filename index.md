I am a lecturer at the [School of Computing](https://www.otago.ac.nz/school-of-computing) at the [University of Otago](https://www.otago.ac.nz) in Dunedin, New Zealand.

My research interests are in mathematical and computational phylogenetics and their intersection with machine learning.
By carefully designing machine learning models based on the structure of phylogenetic data and evolutionary processes, I aim to understand what neural network models can actually learn in this area and where they can be of use in phylogenetic analyses.
